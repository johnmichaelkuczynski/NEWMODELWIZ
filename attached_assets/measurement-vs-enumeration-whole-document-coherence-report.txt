### 2. **Consistency as Binary vs. Graduated**
- **Skeleton 5.11**: "Erratic standards yield inconsistencies; regular standards yield consistency" (binary)
- **Chunk 7**: "consistent with each other to a high degree of approximation" (graduated)
- **TENSION**: The n≠n argument requires *logical* inconsistency, but measurement practice involves *empirical* variance. The text conflates these.

### 3. **Framework Invariance Terminology**
- **Skeleton 5.30**: "Light speed constant relative to any framework F" (relational)
- **Chunk 7**: "Speed of light is framework invariant" (absolute)
- **CONTRADICTION**: Relational constancy ≠ invariance. The former allows different values in different frames; the latter does not.
