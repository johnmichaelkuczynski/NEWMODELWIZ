proposition remains. For example, "The book was given to him by her" versus "She gave
him the book" may suggest different emphases on the giver or the recipient depending on
context or tone.

despite any syntactic shift.
Contextual grounding ensures that reference adapts to situational variables, resolving
ambiguities inherent in natural language. For example, "The bank is closing" could refer to
a financial institution or a riverbank; context clarifies the intended meaning. This
grounding in context allows Ω-Reference to account for the pragmatic aspects of
reference.
These components—referential stability, intentional integrity, and contextual
